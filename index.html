Formula and hair fit

Will this suit my hair?

Most likely, if your scalp gets oily by the end of the day and you'd rather not use a separate conditioner.

A good fit if you have

  • A scalp that feels oily or itchy after a hot day
  • Normal, fine or short hair
  • Hair that sees salt water, pools or a lot of sweat

Maybe not, if you have

  • Very dry, curly or bleached hair that needs a rich conditioner
  • A scalp that dislikes a cooling tingle
  • Colour-treated hair you wash daily

Wash routine

Three steps, then back outside

  1. Step 1

    Pump one or two

    One pump for short hair, two for shoulder length. Work it between wet palms first.

  2. Step 2

    Massage the scalp

    Focus on the roots with your fingertips for 30 seconds. Let the foam run down the lengths on its own.

  3. Step 3

    Rinse well

    Rinse with lukewarm water until hair squeaks lightly. No conditioner needed after.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I still need conditioner?

Most people with normal to fine hair don't. If your ends feel dry, add a light conditioner to the lengths only.

Is it safe for coloured hair?

It's gentle enough for occasional use on coloured hair. If you wash daily, alternate with a colour-care shampoo.

How strong is the menthol?

You'll feel a clear cool tingle for a few minutes after rinsing. It fades without leaving the scalp tight.
