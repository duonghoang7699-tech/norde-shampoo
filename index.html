What's inside

Why it feels so cool

Both bottles are built around the same idea: get the salt and oil off fast, and leave a cool, clean feeling behind.

01

Cool menthol

Menthol triggers the skin's cold receptors, so your scalp and skin feel fresh for a few minutes after rinsing. It's why a wash feels like a reset after the beach.

02

Deep cleansing

Mild cleansers loosen sebum, salt, sunscreen and sweat so they rinse away with water, without leaving skin tight.

03

Rinse in care

In the shampoo, conditioning agents settle on the hair as the foam rinses out, cutting tangles so most people can skip a separate conditioner.

A good fit if you have

  • A scalp that gets oily or itchy after a hot day
  • Normal, fine or short hair
  • Days of salt water, pools or a lot of sweat

Maybe not, if you have

  • Very dry, curly or bleached hair that needs a rich conditioner
  • Skin that dislikes a cooling tingle
  • Colour-treated hair you wash daily

After the beach

Three steps, then back outside

  1. Step 1

    Pump one or two

    One pump of shampoo for short hair, two for shoulder length. Work it between wet palms first.

  2. Step 2

    Scalp, then skin

    Massage the roots for 30 seconds while the body soap does the rest. Let the foam run down on its own.

  3. Step 3

    Rinse cool

    Rinse with lukewarm water until hair squeaks lightly. Feel the menthol kick in. No conditioner needed.

FAQ

Good to know

Do I still need conditioner?

Most people with normal to fine hair don't. If your ends feel dry, add a light conditioner to the lengths only.

Can I use the body soap on my face?

It's made for the body. For the face, use a gentle facial cleanser, and keep menthol away from the eyes.

How strong is the menthol?

You'll feel a clear cool tingle for a few minutes after rinsing. It fades without leaving skin tight.
